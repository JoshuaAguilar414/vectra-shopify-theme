Frequently Asked Questions

Answers about VECTRA Resources and how to find the right material.

Responsible business resources are insights, guides, evidence, and expert analysis that help organizations understand ESG, supply chain, compliance, risk management, business resilience, and operational resilience issues and decide what action to take.
VECTRA Resources are written for procurement, operations, supply chain, risk, compliance, sustainability, and transformation leaders who need to understand an issue before making or defending a decision.
VECTRA International offers Insights, Ebooks and Playbooks, Events and Webinars, VCASTs, Case Studies, and Press Releases. Across these formats, you can explore ESG resources, supply chain resources, business resilience resources, risk management resources, and operational resilience resources.
Yes. VECTRA Resources are free to explore. Some Ebooks, Playbooks, and Events may require registration.
Use the search bar to find resources by topic, regulation, risk, or keyword. You can also browse by format if you already know whether you need a quick Insight, a deeper guide, an expert conversation, or a real-world Case Study.
